# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue or pull request.

1. Preferred: use GitHub private vulnerability reporting. On the repository page open the
   **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and fill in the form.
2. Alternative: email `[CONTACT EMAIL]` with the subject "Toolkit security report".

## What to include

- The affected app (Toolkit shell, Sigma, Pigment, ImageVendor, Placeholder) and version or commit.
- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept. Use test data only.
- Any suggested fix, and whether you intend to disclose publicly and when.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
- We aim to confirm the issue and tell you our plan after triage, and keep you updated until it is fixed.
- We credit reporters in the release notes if they wish.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made for the latest desktop release and the `main` branch. Older releases are
not patched; upgrade to the latest.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption,
access only your own test data, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure,
we will not pursue legal action against you for your research.

## Bounties

There is no bug bounty programme. We are grateful for responsible reports.
