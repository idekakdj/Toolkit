# Policy skeletons

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 auditors expect **written policies that management has approved and that staff
follow**. A policy cannot be generated by software: it has to describe what your organization really
does. These skeletons list the headings and the decisions you must make, with defaults that match how
Toolkit behaves. Complete them, have an authorized person approve them, give each an owner and a
review date, and keep the approval record as audit evidence.

| Policy | Must answer | Toolkit default / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Information security policy** | Objectives, scope, roles, management commitment, how exceptions are approved | Scope: Toolkit service and its hosting environment |
| **Risk management** | How risks are identified, scored, treated and reviewed; who accepts residual risk | Start from `SECURITY-ASSESSMENT.md` "Residual risk"; keep a dated risk register |
| **Access control** | Who may reach servers, secrets and data; joiner/mover/leaver steps; MFA; quarterly access review | No shared admin accounts; keys in a secret store; remove access on departure the same day |
| **Cryptography and key management** | Approved algorithms, key custody, rotation, recovery | AES-256-GCM, TLS 1.2+, bcrypt; rotate `TOOLKIT_DATA_KEY` yearly (`OPERATIONS.md` s.4) |
| **Secure development and change management** | Code review, testing gates, approvals, emergency changes, separation of test and production data | `npm test` must pass; tests use synthetic data in temporary directories |
| **Vulnerability and patch management** | Scan cadence, severity-to-deadline table, how exceptions are recorded | Weekly `npm audit`; critical within 7 days, high within 14, others within 30 |
| **Logging and monitoring** | What is logged, retention, who reviews and how often, alert thresholds | Audit log, 365 days, weekly review, off-host copy |
| **Backup and business continuity** | RPO/RTO targets, backup scope and location, restore testing, disaster scenarios | Daily off-host backup; quarterly restore test; document RPO/RTO |
| **Incident response** | Roles, severity levels, communication, breach assessment, record keeping | `OPERATIONS.md` s.6, including PIPEDA breach reporting |
| **Privacy and data protection** | Purposes, consent, retention, individual-rights handling, privacy officer, complaints, cross-border transfers, privacy impact assessments for new features | `public/privacy.html`, `OPERATIONS.md` s.5 and s.7 |
| **Data classification and handling** | Classes (public, internal, confidential, personal), handling rules per class | User artwork and account data are confidential/personal |
| **Vendor and third-party management** | Due diligence, contracts, monitoring, offboarding | Register: hosting provider, mail provider, DNS, npm packages (`VENDOR.md` for vendored files; lamejs is LGPL-3.0, retain its notice) |
| **Acceptable use and personnel security** | Staff responsibilities, screening, onboarding/offboarding, security awareness training (annual) | Needs HR process and training records |
| **Physical and environmental security** | Datacentre or office controls | Inherited from the hosting provider (obtain their SOC 2 or ISO report) |

## Evidence auditors will ask for

Approved policies with dates; the risk register; the access list and its quarterly review; change
records (pull requests and approvals); proof of the weekly vulnerability scans and fixes; restore-test
records; training completion; incident and breach logs; vendor reviews; a penetration test report;
and, for SOC 2 Type II, samples across the whole observation window (commonly 3 to 12 months).
